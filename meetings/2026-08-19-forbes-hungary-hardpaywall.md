## 2. Priority (A) — Big picture: the road to 6,500 subscribers

**Target: 6,500 paying subscribers by the end of 2026.**

The Q4 plan has to be built as one model with three stacked layers. Each layer
answers a different question, and the three of them together have to add up to
6,500 — not approximately, explicitly.

### A.I — Q4 renewals: three scenarios

Model the Q4 renewal base under three renewal rates. For each one we want to see
**how many subscribers we keep and how many we lose** — in absolute numbers, not
only percentages.

| Scenario | Renewal rate | Kept | Lost | Per 1,000 up for renewal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base (current) | 60% | 0.60 × base | 0.40 × base | 600 kept / 400 lost |
| Downside 1 | 53% | 0.53 × base | 0.47 × base | 530 kept / 470 lost |
| Downside 2 | 45% | 0.45 × base | 0.55 × base | 450 kept / 550 lost |

Holding the current 60% is the working assumption; 53% and 45% are there so we
know what breaks and how early we would see it. Please fill in the actual Q4
renewal base so the table becomes real numbers.

### A.II — Softpaywall registrations converting to paid: three conversion alternatives

On top of the renewal layer, add the volume coming from the softpaywall
registered base. Model **three conversion alternatives** (conservative / expected
/ stretch) applied to the registered pool, and show for each how many paying
subscribers it contributes in Q4.

We need two inputs stated plainly: the size of the registered pool available for
conversion in Q4, and the conversion rate assumed in each alternative.

### A.III — New-new: the upside layer

Third layer: subscribers who are **neither subscribers today nor in the
softpaywall registered base**. This is genuinely the hardest number to estimate,
so we treat it as **upside**, not as plan — but it still has to be quantified.

Useful way to read it: the upside is the residual. Once A.I and A.II are on the
table, whatever is missing to 6,500 is what new-new has to deliver — and the
weaker the renewal scenario, the larger that residual becomes. That relationship
is what we want to see on one page.

### Target mix by pillar

|---|---|---|
| Friends of Forbes | 50–60% | 3,250 – 3,900 |

**Note to resolve:** as stated, the two ranges can already consume 100% (60% +
40%), which would leave Insider at zero. Please come back with a pinned mix — a
sensible working center is Friends of Forbes 55% (≈3,575), Digital 35% (≈2,275),
Insider 10% (≈650) — so the pillar targets are unambiguous before the GTM work
is built on top of them.

### Deliverable for 9 September

- **alternative options**, i.e. how we boost that pillar if the primary plan is
  not performing, with the trigger point at which we switch.
